# OWS Hub

OWS Hub: the official desktop launcher and companion app for Ocean & Wild Studios — your single entry point to the entire OWS universe.

OWS Hub brings together everything Ocean & Wild Studios has to offer in one place. Browse the latest news and quick updates, discover new game releases, follow upcoming events, download and install titles with a single click, and keep every game and the Hub itself up to date through the built-in update manager. It also ships with Surface, the OWS wallet module, where players can manage their in-ecosystem currency, send and receive funds between players, and use a virtual card.

Built as a native Windows desktop application (Tauri v2 + Rust), OWS Hub is free to download and designed to be lightweight, fast, and always in sync with the OWS backend. Whether you are a new player taking your first steps or a veteran keeping your library current, OWS Hub keeps you connected to the world of Ocean & Wild Studios.

## Highlights

- **News & Quick News** — official announcements, patches, and short updates, refreshed in real time.
- **Releases** — full catalog of OWS games with status, version, and one-click download.
- **Events** — calendar of updates, launches, and community events with live status.
- **Changelogs** — version notes for the Hub and every published game.
- **Downloads Manager** — live progress, install, launch, and uninstall your games.
- **Updates Manager** — keep OWS Hub and your installed games on the latest version.
- **Surface** — the OWS wallet: balances, transfers between players, and a virtual card.
- **Admin Panel** — owner-only tools for managing news, events, projects, and the Hub itself.

## Platform

Windows (desktop). OWS Hub is required to download, install, and launch OWS games from the browser experience.
